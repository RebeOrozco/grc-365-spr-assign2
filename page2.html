Unforgettable Nights

Lady Gaga officially unleashed "The Mayhem Ball" at the T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas on July 16, 2025, marking a spectacular return to the stage with her most theatrical production to date. The opening night was a masterclass in avant-garde storytelling, featuring a Colosseum-inspired stage and a show-stopping crimson "cage dress" that housed a troupe of dancers during the performance.
